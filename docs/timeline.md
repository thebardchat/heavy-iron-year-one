# Heavy Iron Year One — Execution Timeline

> ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-09-01:** GSAxcess.gov was decommissioned in 2023. Acquisition now goes through **PPMS.gov**, applied via **ADECA** (Alabama's State Agency for Surplus Property), after VetCert approval. The mandatory hold period before title vests is **18 months**, not 12 — "Day 366" throughout this timeline has been corrected to Month 18. Full sourcing: CLAUDE.md's Acquisition Program Correction section. Specific dollar figures below are illustrative estimates carried over from the old GSAxcess-era model, not verified against a real ADECA quote.

## Phase 1 — Formation (Weeks 1–4)
Get legal and business structure locked before applying to the surplus property program.

- [ ] Form LLC or corporation
- [ ] Get EIN from IRS.gov
- [ ] Open business bank account
- [ ] Draft & sign LLC Operating Agreement — Josh 51%/Shane 49% ownership, 50/50 profit split (Option B), before first machine purchase
- [ ] Attorney review — the 18-month mandatory-use restriction (41 CFR Part 102-37, property $5,000+)
- [ ] Register on SAM.gov
- [ ] Apply for VetCert at veteransbusinesscertification.va.gov (60–90 day clock starts NOW)

> **Why this order:** VetCert takes 60–90 days, and it's required before ADECA will register you. File it on Day 1. Everything else waits on it but the clock starts immediately. The Operating Agreement should be signed before Phase 3 — never buy the first machine on a handshake.

## Phase 3 — First Acquisition (Weeks 4–8)
Once LLC is formed, bank account is open, and VetCert is approved.

- [ ] Register with ADECA Surplus Property Division (adeca.alabama.gov/surplus/) once VetCert is approved
- [ ] Research Cat 320 availability via PPMS.gov / ADECA
- [ ] Get a real service-charge quote from ADECA before budgeting — do not assume the old ~15%-of-replacement-cost estimate holds
- [ ] Calculate acquisition budget (illustrative estimate only: ~$34,500 — unverified, confirm with ADECA)
- [ ] Submit acquisition request through ADECA
- [ ] Arrange transport to storage on acquisition

> **Hold period note:** the 18-month mandatory-use restriction begins on acquisition date — corrected from the old "1-year" assumption. Day 1 of the clock = Day 1 of revenue either way. Work the machine hard.

## Phase 4 — Government Contracting (Weeks 6–12)
Can start before VetCert arrives — build the pipeline now.

- [ ] Complete SAM.gov profile 100%
- [ ] Draft capability statement (1-page SDVOSB intro doc)
- [ ] Identify 3 local government bid opportunities
- [ ] Register on beta.SAM.gov for bid alerts
- [ ] Submit first government bid

> **VetCert unlocks:** SDVOSB sole-source up to $4.5M (services) — VA legally required to use SDVOSBs first. This is the moat.

## Phase 5 — Revenue & Scale (Month 3–18)
Machine is working. Money is coming in. Eyes on the flywheel — the cycle is now 18 months, not 12.

- [ ] Hit $80,000 service net target by Month 12 (run-rate; the machine keeps earning through Month 18)
- [ ] Track machine hours and maintenance
- [ ] Document all revenue for tax return (Section 179 play)
- [ ] Begin identifying Cycle 2 acquisition targets (2–3 machines) around Month 12–15
- [ ] At Month 18 — title vests; sell machine at market (illustrative: ~$92,000, unverified)
- [ ] Reinvest proceeds into Cycle 2 fleet
