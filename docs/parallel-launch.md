# Heavy Iron Year One — Parallel Launch Plan

**Purpose:** [`timeline.md`](./timeline.md) lays out Year One as five sequential phases. This doc resequences the same plan into **concurrent tracks** so Site Services (Proposal 1), the Training Academy (Proposal 2), and Dirt & Materials (Proposal 3) can all be stood up at once instead of waiting on each other. One flywheel, three business fronts — this is how to work all three without one blocking the others.

Source of truth for numbers/strategy: [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md). This doc doesn't change the strategy — it only resequences execution for parallelism. Legal and tax items are flagged for the attorney and CPA, not final advice.

> ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-09-01:** GSAxcess.gov was decommissioned in 2023 — acquisition now goes through PPMS.gov via ADECA (Alabama's State Agency for Surplus Property), and the mandatory hold period is 18 months, not the "Day 366" figure referenced below. See CLAUDE.md's Acquisition Program Correction section.

## Track B — Training Academy (Proposal 2)
Owner: Josh (instructor path) + Shane (systems/enrollment)

This is the slowest-moving track — accreditation and licensure lead time. Start it in Week 1, not after Track A lands.

1. Confirm state equipment-operator licensure requirement/timeline
2. Apply for NCCER accreditation (~$500–$2,000/yr) — needs Josh's NCCER Craft Instructor credential in motion
3. Apply to Alabama SAA for GI Bill approval (2–6 months after state licensure — start the clock now)
4. Apply for WIOA/ETPL listing ($5K–$15K per student in Individual Training Accounts)
5. Build curriculum + cohort structure (target: 10 students × $12K × 4 cohorts = $480K/yr model)
6. Identify training site (can share space/equipment with Track A in Year 1)

Target: first cohort in market by the time the first machine's revenue is proven (Month 3–4), so the Combo model's numbers hold.

## Why parallel instead of sequential
The old phase-by-phase timeline made sense for a single machine, single revenue stream. Running the Combo model ($350,100 Year 1 net) means Track A and Track B have to be live in the same year, not staged one after another — Track B's accreditation lead time (NCCER + GI Bill + WIOA) is the long pole, so it has to start on Day 1 alongside Track 0, not after the first machine is already making money.
